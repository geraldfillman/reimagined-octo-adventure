# Macro Snapshot Log

A tiny, durable, **append-only** time series for the macro lens. One block per month capturing the regime call and the conviction of each macro-core thesis. Unlike pull notes (which age out to `World_Machine` under retention), this log is never pruned — it is the long-horizon record of how the macro book evolved.

**How to use:** once a month (or after a material regime shift), copy the template block, date it, and fill it from [[00_Dashboard/Macro Lens Board]]. Keep notes to one line each. See [[04_Reference/Macro Lens]].
