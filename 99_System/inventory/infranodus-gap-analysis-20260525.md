## What This Is

The daily `knowledge-gap-tasks` cadence generates freshness gaps (stale pull dates).
This analysis finds **structural gaps** — topics that exist in the queue but are
weakly connected to the rest of the knowledge network. These are blind spots that
freshness tracking cannot detect.
