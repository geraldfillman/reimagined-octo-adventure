# Igloo Research Studio and Faculty Project Accelerator

[[00 CyberSickness Hub|Back to hub]]  /  [[03 Ranked Project Portfolio]]  /  [[Sources/Evidence Register]]

> [!success] Portfolio decision
> **Rank 2  /  89.5/100  /  Build now**  
> Run one clinical or decision-learning cycle; do not launch a broad lab program before capacity is measured.

## Target user

Faculty investigators, simulation educators, immersive-learning staff, and research-support partners who have a real teaching or research question but not a production pipeline.

## Problem

The archive contains many strong ideas but repeated stalls between concept, reliable delivery, evaluation, approvals, and transition. One-off custom builds also overload the operator.

## Existing assets

- Reviewed partnership agreement with intake, stages, capacity, and transition.
- Existing template requests, shared-room clinical scenarios, and a completed HERT-style implementation artifact.
- Reviewed platform roadmap and reusable production specifications.
- A funding precedent that explicitly values XR/non-XR comparison and cybersickness mitigation.

## Differentiator

The studio sells a completed evidence cycle--not just content: one question, one reusable implementation, one facilitator package, one measurement plan, one fallback, and one transition decision.

## Minimum pilot

Choose one existing low-motion clinical or decision scenario. Run a four-week studio cycle producing intake, objectives, session pack, facilitator guide, preflight, comfort/accessibility plan, pilot, debrief, and evidence summary.

## Business or funding route

Institutional project sponsorship, educational innovation funds, research subawards, or a bounded design-and-evaluation service. A broader studio identity follows only after capacity and reuse are demonstrated.

## Dependencies

- named faculty owner
- one primary outcome
- room readiness
- approved content and data plan
- facilitator and fallback
- capacity ceiling

## Evidence gaps

- actual staff hours per stage
- transfer ownership after pilot
- funding calendar
- objective outcome data
- cross-site reuse cost

## Risks

- intake becomes an unlimited request queue
- enthusiasm is mistaken for efficacy
- advanced media eclipses the learning objective
- support load is hidden

## Success measures

- cycle lead time
- hours by role and stage
- pilot completion rate
- reuse without original builder
- facilitator readiness
- learning/comfort data completeness
- transition decision

## Path forward

### First 7 days

- Publish a one-page intake rubric and three complexity tiers.
- Select one existing scenario and name the decision owner.
- Freeze scope, comparator or baseline, primary outcome, comfort measure, fallback, and capacity budget.

### By day 30

- Run one full studio cycle.
- Capture operator, faculty, facilitator, and learner evidence.
- Package the reusable outputs and write an honest after-action report.

### By day 90

- Repeat the same model in two additional programs, not three new technologies.
- Compare cycle cost and reuse quality.
- Choose internal program, funded research service, or external implementation offer based on evidence.
