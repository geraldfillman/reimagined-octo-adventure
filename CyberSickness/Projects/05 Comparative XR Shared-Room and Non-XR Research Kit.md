## Target user

Simulation researchers and educators comparing headset XR, shared room-scale projection, and non-XR instruction.

## Problem

The archive repeatedly proposes comparative studies, but modalities are sometimes conflated, comfort measures are inconsistently specified, and grant readiness trails idea generation.

## Existing assets

- A user-requested family of bounded XR/non-XR study ideas.
- Completed shared-room clinical work that could anchor a follow-up.
- Scenario, survey, and facilitation material across several clinical domains.
- A current literature rationale for standardized measurement and washout planning.

## Differentiator

The kit treats headset XR, shared projection, and ordinary simulation as separate interventions and pairs objective learning outcomes with comfort, accessibility, exposure, facilitator, and reliability evidence.

## Minimum pilot

Select one existing scenario and one comparison only. Draft a protocol, outcome hierarchy, comfort battery, exposure/sensory description, stop rule, washout approach, fallback, data dictionary, and analysis plan before recruitment.

## Business or funding route

Simulation research award, internal seed grant, faculty partnership, or a research-support package. The cited 2026 funding instructions are precedent, not proof of a currently open round.

## Dependencies

- principal investigator
- IRB determination
- one comparator
- validated measures
- sample-size/analysis support
- room reliability
- data owner

## Evidence gaps

- objective outcome selection
- sample and power assumptions
- current funding deadline
- validated shared-room comfort approach
- replication plan

## Risks

- underpowered multi-arm design
- modality labels are imprecise
- novelty effects dominate
- washout is inadequate
- operational failures become unrecorded exclusions

## Success measures

- protocol completeness
- recruitment and completion
- learning/skill outcome
- comfort and adverse events
- exposure and break adherence
- missing-data rate
- reproducibility package

## Path forward

### First 7 days

- Choose one scenario, population, comparator, and primary outcome.
- Name investigator, data owner, statistician/methods reviewer, and operator.
- Draft the common comfort/accessibility and reliability fields.

### By day 30

- Complete protocol, instruments, data dictionary, analysis skeleton, and approvals path.
- Run a technical rehearsal without research participants.
- Prepare a funder-ready one-page rationale and honest feasibility budget.

### By day 90

- Obtain approval and run a feasibility cohort, or submit the bounded funding packet.
- Publish protocol deviations and operational failures alongside outcomes.
- Decide whether a larger comparison is justified.
