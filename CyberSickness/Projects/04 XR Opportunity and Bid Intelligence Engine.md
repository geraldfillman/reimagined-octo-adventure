# XR Opportunity and Bid Intelligence Engine

[[00 CyberSickness Hub|Back to hub]]  /  [[03 Ranked Project Portfolio]]  /  [[Sources/Evidence Register]]

> [!success] Portfolio decision
> **Rank 4  /  83.5/100  /  Build now**  
> Use it internally to qualify one real opportunity; do not sell a generic RFP product yet.

## Target user

A small XR/immersive-learning studio or institutional partnership team deciding where to invest limited capture and proposal effort.

## Problem

Opportunity research is scattered, stale, and easily biased toward interesting technology instead of winnable, active, mission-aligned work.

## Existing assets

- A reviewed active/unassigned SAM.gov search specification.
- Conversation history defining an XR studio, public-sector contract research, and internal capture engine.
- Reusable ingestion, provenance, scoring, and Obsidian reporting patterns.
- An official public API for opportunities with daily active-notice updates.

## Differentiator

The engine is not a generic alert feed. It encodes real immersive-learning capabilities, room/platform constraints, partner needs, evidence requirements, and a human bid/no-bid gate.

## Minimum pilot

Run one live search taxonomy, normalize and deduplicate active notices, qualify 25, and produce one bid/no-bid packet with source URLs, dates, eligibility, scope fit, gaps, partners, and next action.

## Business or funding route

Internal business-development leverage first; then a managed research/capture service for a narrow XR or education niche if repeated external demand appears.

## Path forward

### First 7 days

- Refresh capability statement and exclusion rules.
- Configure and dry-run the active-opportunity taxonomy.
- Define a one-page bid/no-bid packet.

### By day 30

- Qualify 25 current notices with primary-source links.
- Complete one packet and one partner conversation.
- Record false positives and taxonomy changes.

### By day 90

- Reach one submitted, sponsored, or paid pilot--or explicitly revise/stop the niche.
- Automate only repeated classification steps.
- Keep final eligibility and bid decisions human-owned.
