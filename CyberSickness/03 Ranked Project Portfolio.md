## Rubric and mechanics

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 5 in half-point increments. Weighted points are `customer x 5 + readiness x 4 + differentiation x 4 + 30-day pilot x 3 + archive evidence x 2 + compounding x 2`, producing a 0-100 total. Tiers are mechanical: 75-100 Build now; 60-74 Validate next; 45-59 Incubate; below 45 Park. A disposition may still narrow or merge a high-scoring concept, but it does not change the mechanical tier.

## Portfolio tiers and treatment

### Build now

Build-now means **run a bounded pilot**, not build a company or platform. The top five have individual implementation briefs. Rugby also clears the mechanical threshold but sits behind the central compound pilot because buyer access, footage rights, and workflow fit remain unresolved.

### Validate next

Patent research, a standalone comfort toolkit, AV/facilitation patterns, clinical scenario packs, decision games, quant agents, and documentary templates all have credible assets. Most should be merged into the top operating program or tested through interviews and one artifact before software expansion.

### Incubate and park

The broad virtual-hospital/all-subject platform mechanically lands in Incubate, but the recommended disposition is to park it because later user decisions repeatedly narrow the scope. Revisit only after three narrow modules demonstrate demand, reuse, and support economics.

## Recommended portfolio sequence

1. **Days 1-7:** establish the operator baseline and known-good session; select one faculty scenario; freeze a single comparator and quality fields; select one nursing calculation and one SME; refresh the XR capability statement.
2. **Days 8-30:** run one compound studio/research rehearsal, ship one browser nursing module if capacity permits, and qualify current opportunities into one bid/no-bid packet.
3. **Days 31-90:** repeat only what worked across another program or room; seek one funded/sponsored outcome; require evidence before adding technologies or catalogs.

## Revisit triggers

- Rugby moves up after three coach interviews, footage permission, and one manual replay session.
- Patent-to-Product moves up after ten buyer interviews plus a paid pilot or formal legal partner.
- The comfort toolkit becomes standalone only after repeated collected datasets and a clear institutional buyer.
- Decision games move up after a playable slice produces learner and distribution evidence.
- Quant tooling becomes commercial only after one fixed, reproducible out-of-sample result after costs.
- A broad platform reopens only after three narrow modules are reused without the original builder.
