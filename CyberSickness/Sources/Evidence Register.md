## Evidence classes

- **User-originated direction/report:** evidence of the user's intent, constraints, correction, or reported experience; it is not automatically independently verified.
- **Assistant-generated proposal/synthesis:** evidence that an idea or claim appeared in the archive; never treated as established fact without a current primary source or pilot.
- **Attached artifact:** evidence from a specific exported file. The register states whether text/content was reviewed; generated artifacts remain proposals unless implementation is independently verified.
- **Externally verified fact:** a bounded current claim tied to an official, standards, or peer-reviewed primary source and access date. Vendor case studies are not used as independent efficacy proof.
