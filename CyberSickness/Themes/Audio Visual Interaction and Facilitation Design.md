# Audio, visual, interaction, and facilitation design

[[00 CyberSickness Hub|Back to hub]]  /  [[02 Theme Map]]  /  [[Sources/Evidence Register]]

> [!summary] Interpretation
> Audio, imagery, projection geometry, interaction, and facilitation are not polish; they are instructional and operational variables that shape attention, stress, group coordination, and comfort.

## What the history says

- The shared-room clinical example deliberately combines looping imagery, multichannel noise, PPE, physical equipment, and live role-play to create environmental pressure.
- White Rose work develops archival audio, timeline, scene, and projection practices that can become reusable documentary patterns.
- Audience interaction and operator routing show that facilitation requires controlled parameters, legible state, synchronization, and safe scene changes.
- Seam-safe graphics, frame rate, audio drift, and sensory intensity belong in both technical acceptance tests and research descriptions.

## Current reality check

- [Igloo WebView documentation](https://docs.igloovision.com/documentation/current/webview) -- WebView uses Chromium and supports web content including WebGL/WebRTC, with configurable rendering settings. Accessed 2026-07-30. (official vendor documentation)
- [W3C WebXR Device API](https://www.w3.org/TR/webxr/) -- WebXR remains a W3C Candidate Recommendation Draft and therefore should be treated as a browser capability with compatibility testing, not a frozen standard. Accessed 2026-07-30. (primary standards document)
- [HMD content and exposure factors associated with cybersickness](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32300295/) -- Published evidence associates sickness with content, locomotion, and exposure duration, supporting explicit media and exposure logging. Accessed 2026-07-30. (peer-reviewed article indexed by PubMed)

## Evidence gaps and next tests

- Archive material rarely separates intended cognitive load from unintended discomfort in measurement.
- Rights and licensing remain unresolved for some archival media concepts.
- A reusable facilitation rubric and sensory-intensity descriptor have not yet been operationalized.
