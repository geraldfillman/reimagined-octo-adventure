# Patent discovery, products, contracts, grants, and commercialization

[[00 CyberSickness Hub|Back to hub]]  /  [[02 Theme Map]]  /  [[Sources/Evidence Register]]

> [!summary] Interpretation
> Commercialization repeatedly converges on service first: solve one domain problem manually, deliver a provenance-backed packet, automate repeated work internally, and expose software only after demand is observed.

## What the history says

- Patent work moves from curiosity and possible alpha toward demand-first failure analysis, deterministic evidence, and legal quarantine.
- Procurement work narrows from a generic RFP scout to an internal XR/education opportunity engine with bid/no-bid discipline.
- The studio concept supplies the commercial umbrella: services fund reusable tools and evidence, while platform claims wait for repetition.
- Contracts, grants, and research awards are routes--not proof of revenue. Every current opportunity, deadline, and eligibility claim needs live verification.

## Current reality check

- [SAM.gov Get Opportunities public API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/) -- GSA documents an opportunities API with API-key and pagination requirements and daily updates to active notices, enabling a reproducible opportunity-monitoring workflow. Accessed 2026-07-30. (official U.S. government API documentation)
- [USPTO Open Data Portal registration notice](https://www.uspto.gov/about-us/news-updates/uspto-open-data-portal-require-registration-access-beginning-june-18-2026) -- USPTO requires Open Data Portal registration beginning June 18, 2026, a current operational constraint for PatentAgent-style tooling. Accessed 2026-07-30. (official U.S. government notice)
- [USPTO patent search resources](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/search) -- USPTO provides the authoritative starting point for patent search and status resources; legal conclusions still require qualified review. Accessed 2026-07-30. (official U.S. government resource)
- [SSH Fund research awards](https://www.ssih.org/ssh-fund/impact/research-awards) -- The fund maintains a research-award program relevant to simulation research; current deadlines require a fresh check before submission planning. Accessed 2026-07-30. (official funder page)

## Evidence gaps and next tests

- No confirmed paying buyer for patent packets or Igloo operations as a standalone offer.
- Patent status and freedom-to-operate conclusions require attorney review and cannot be inferred from search output.
- Opportunity qualification needs a capability statement, bid threshold, partner map, and one real submission test.
