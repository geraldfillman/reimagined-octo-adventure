# Quant finance, probability, and decision-literacy experiences

[[00 CyberSickness Hub|Back to hub]]  /  [[02 Theme Map]]  /  [[Sources/Evidence Register]]

> [!summary] Interpretation
> The durable quant idea is decision literacy--making uncertainty, risk, incentives, and consequences visible--not selling an autonomous trading system.

## What the history says

- Probability dashboards, Markov labs, expected-value ladders, rugby rules, medication calculations, and games all use the same scenario-to-decision-to-feedback loop.
- Later conversations demand assessment gates, luck-adjusted scoring, limitations, and out-of-sample testing, which are stronger than early visualization-first concepts.
- The research-agent work is useful as internal evidence infrastructure until a single signal survives a fixed validation protocol after costs.
- Decision games need one playable lesson and a distribution test before a studio-scale content plan.
