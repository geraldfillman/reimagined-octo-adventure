# AI agents, automation, and reusable operator tools

[[00 CyberSickness Hub|Back to hub]]  /  [[02 Theme Map]]  /  [[Sources/Evidence Register]]

> [!summary] Interpretation
> Across XR operations, markets, patents, and procurement, the same useful spine repeats: ingest, normalize, preserve provenance, score, route to human review, publish a decision artifact, and refresh on a schedule.

## What the history says

- The most valuable automation is bounded and auditable; it supports an operator decision rather than claiming autonomous expertise.
- Igloo tooling has unusually concrete artifacts: health-check concepts, mini-project specs, session packs, audience input, routing, and platform roadmaps.
- The archive repeatedly rejects technology for its own sake, including broad Kubernetes or generic research-OS framing, in favor of a narrow vertical workflow.
- One-person scalability depends on read-only checks, explicit remediation gates, reusable outputs, and fallback paths.

## Evidence gaps and next tests

- Generated artifacts are specifications, not proof of production deployment.
- Credential storage, access control, audit retention, and incident ownership need local governance.
- A generic AI platform has no validated buyer; automation should remain embedded in vertical services.
